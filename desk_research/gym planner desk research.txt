-----------------
FitnessAI: https://www.fitnessai.com/
-----------------

Key design elements that inspired the project:

Clean and modern layout
- The website uses a minimal design with plenty of white space, making the interface easy to read and visually appealing.

Efficient progress tracking
- FitnessAI focuses on tracking sets, reps, and strength progression, which inspired the idea of including progress tracking features in the gym planner.

Simple navigation and user flow
- The design makes it easy for users to quickly start a workout or view their progress, influencing the idea of creating a clear dashboard for workouts.

Mobile-friendly design
- The layout adapts well to mobile devices, which inspired the structure for mobile.

-----------------
Muscle & Strength: https://www.muscleandstrength.com/workout-routines
-----------------

Key design elements that inspired the project:

Structured workout plans
Workout routines are organised clearly with sets, reps, and instructions, influencing the structured workout logging system in the planner.

Visual exercise guidance
Many exercises include images or videos, which inspired the idea of adding exercise demonstrations to help users perform movements correctly.

Content-focused layout
The site prioritises useful fitness information relative to users, which influenced the decision to make the planner simple and focused on functionality rather than cluttered design.

-----------------
Summary
-----------------

Both websites helped influence the design of the gym planner:

- FitnessAI inspired the modern interface, progress tracking, and mobile usability.
- Muscle & Strength inspired the structured routines, and workout guidance.
Combining these ideas helped shape a gym planner that is easy to use, visually clean, and useful for tracking workouts and planning training sessions.
